# Analytical report standard

This reference defines the editorial criteria shared by report authors and isolated reviewers through `src/editorial-standard.js`. The examples below inform the criteria; they are neither a ranking of institutions nor evidence for a research project's conclusions. Automated review can reject incomplete analysis but does not certify professional quality or factual truth.

These are targeted readings of the specified sections, not a claim to have read every report cover to cover. Borrow analytical discipline, not promotional language, proprietary scores or a universal consulting outline.

## Composition and review

Research starts with question-level design: the decision affected, plausible alternatives, observations that distinguish them and the stopping condition for a bounded answer. Each saved question assessment states its answer, reasoning, alternatives considered, remaining gaps and next investigation. An institution's presence or a linked quotation does not establish how an advantage formed. A defensible negative or conditional finding can answer a question; an unanswered question remains unresolved.

Corroborated claims retain the project's independent-source requirement. Explicit attribution establishes what an identified source says, while an internal consistency check can establish an arithmetic or textual discrepancy within one original. Neither limited judgment establishes real-world effectiveness or a causal generalization. Review examines the actual claim and its conditions, including factual statements introduced in paragraphs, captions and tables.

The isolated reviewer receives all acquired text originals, including material omitted from the author's selected citations. Negative chapter findings may refer to any saved evidence in the run; passing findings require chapter-linked support. Original-figure and visual-observation review requires actual image input. Text extraction and the author's visual notes cannot substitute for independent inspection. Capability failures require a suitable host or model, rather than repeated text-only reviews.

Resolve review findings through the appropriate research operation: acquire missing observations, establish a common comparison basis or revise the explanation. Then rewrite the affected argument and synthesis together. Adding disconnected caveats does not resolve an unsupported mechanism. Collection, question analysis, semantic verification and report readiness remain separate assessments.

The executive synthesis states the answer, supporting observations, implications and decisive uncertainty. Body chapters develop these findings with facts, comparisons, mechanisms and evidence; repeating a takeaway in a paragraph does not provide analysis. The method identifies scope, date cutoff and comparison criteria. References and collection records remain available outside the main narrative.

Use developed prose and meaningful comparisons, with sourced charts or relevant original pages where they add evidence. Explain useful relationships in the body and remove redundant relationship diagrams. Illustration types have no quota. Cite saved evidence at the relevant sentence with `{{evidence:EVIDENCE_ID}}`; the reader renders clickable superscripts. Depth means explaining mechanisms, concrete examples, alternatives, limits and decision implications for the requested questions; repetition and word-count padding do not supply it.

Exhibits answer a specific analytical question. Captions state bounded findings; nearby notes identify sources, units, periods, samples and caveats as applicable. Tables compare common dimensions, charts show compatible observations, and the body explains dependencies or failure conditions. A chain of generic boxes is not an exhibit. Original-source diagrams need an explanation of their relevance and limits.

Conclusions distinguish observations from inference. Recommendations identify actions, prerequisites and signals that could change the decision. Missing evidence limits the affected conclusion rather than disappearing into a generic limitations paragraph. A technical report need not contain market sizing or investment recommendations.

The isolated reviewer applies the same criteria and records chapter-specific excerpts, supporting evidence and concrete revisions. Structural validation checks the references and completeness of this assessment; semantic judgments still depend on the reviewing model. Tests of prompt delivery and receipt validation are not evaluations of real report quality.
